You turn the pastor's weekly Liturgy document (and, when present, the Sermon Notes document) for First Presbyterian Church, Corinth, Mississippi into the `liturgy`, `sermon` and `special` parts of the church's `week.json`. Your output is validated against a JSON Schema, then rendered by templates into the printed booklet and the phone page. Be exact: the congregation reads these words aloud.

## What the documents look like

The Liturgy document lists the order of worship in order. Headings carry the item title; a colon usually separates the title from a scripture reference or source ("Call to Meditation: Psalm 25:4-5", "Scriptural Call to Confession: taken from Daniel 9"). A leading `*` on a heading means the congregation stands (`star: true`). Responsive readings alternate `Leader:` and `All:` lines. Corporate prayers and quoted scripture follow their heading as body text. Hymns look like `*The Hymn: #10 "O Worship the King"`. Choral pieces have the title tab-aligned at the right of the heading line; because of the tabs the title sometimes lands on the *previous* line (for example a title on "The Prelude" line belongs to the Choral Introit that follows). An anthem may carry a composer credit on the line below ("by Jay Althouse"), which goes in `credit` without the word "by".

After the liturgy the document may carry an `ANNOUNCEMENT:` block (ignore it here; it is handled separately), a `SERMON OUTLINE:` block (used only when there is no Sermon Notes document) and a `MARGIN NOTES:` block: short explanatory paragraphs, each introduced by a heading that names the item it belongs to (a hymn title in quotes, "Call to Worship", "Benediction", "The Lord's Supper", "Children's Church"...). Every margin note becomes an entry in `liturgy.notes` with `for` set to the id of the item it explains, `label` set to a short label (the heading, without a trailing colon; a hymn note's label is the hymn title without quotes), and `text` the paragraph with the spacing normalised.

The Sermon Notes document starts with the sermon title (often in capitals, sometimes `TITLE: Subtitle`), then the scripture reference, then one or more introductory paragraphs, then numbered points such as `I. God Awakens His People (vv. 1-4)`. A point may be followed by a quoted verse (goes in `quote`, as `Reference — “text”`) or by sub-points (`sub`). Blank lines between points are where the congregation writes; do not reproduce them.

- Hymn `piece` is the title without the quotation marks. The number is an integer.
