Welcome

We Are Glad You Are Here!

We are honored that you decided to join us this morning. Whether it is your first time in church, your first time in a long while, or part of your regular rhythm, we hope you will be encouraged by the fellowship with each other, and with our Lord, as we worship him together.

If this is your first time with us, please take a moment and fill out a Next Steps Card found on the back of the pew, or scan the QR code and submit it on your phone. We would love to meet you and help you get connected.

Children & Nursery

Children (age 4+) and their families worship together in the sanctuary, where children will be dismissed to Children’s Church during the service. Children’s bulletins and crayons are available at both entrances to the Sanctuary. Children are always welcome in worship!

Nursery rooms for infants through age 3 are in the Education Wing, with the entrance behind the playground on the west side of campus. If you have a newborn and/or are nursing your baby, please feel free to use our baby room just off the narthex — a quiet space with comfortable seating where the worship service is broadcast as well.

Our Vision & Mission

Our vision is to see lives transformed by the power of the gospel, rooted in God’s Word and the Reformed tradition, in order to create a culture of discipleship at FPC where disciples WORSHIP holistically, GROW relationally in Christ, and SERVE boldly in the world for the glory of God. Our mission is to accomplish this by “Being Disciples and Making Disciples.”

Sermon Notes

“Spiritual Renewal: A Shared Calling”

Rev. Waring Porter  ·  Nehemiah 3

In chapter 2, Nehemiah challenges the people to rise up and build. The people respond with enthusiasm saying, “Let us rise up and build.” Now, in chapter 3, we see what that response actually looks like.

We see the priests, the goldsmiths, the perfume makers, the merchants, the rulers, and even the daughters of one of the leaders all participating in the work. As you read through the chapter, one phrase keeps appearing: “Next to him…” One person works on one section of the wall, and somebody else comes alongside and works on the next section. It’s a beautiful picture of God’s people working together.

God’s work is not the responsibility of a few particularly gifted or committed individuals. It is a shared calling of His entire people. It was true of the rebuilding of Jerusalem, and it is still true for the church today. There are three things this passage teaches us about our shared calling as the people of God.

I. Requires Everyone (vv. 1–12)

II. Requires Faithfulness (vv. 13–27)

Ephesians 4:15–16 — “we are to grow up in every way into him who is the head, into Christ, from whom the whole body, joined and held together by every joint with which it is equipped, when each part is working properly, makes the body grow so that it builds itself up in love.”

III. Requires a Common Commitment (vv. 28–32)

The Sacrament of The Lord’s Supper

The Lord’s Supper The Lord’s Supper is a means of grace in which Christ spiritually nourishes believers, strengthening their faith as they partake of the bread and cup in union with Him. We reflect on how thankful we are for the work of Christ on our behalf, and we pray that his grace would work more powerfully in our lives to change us more into his likeness. The sacrament is a tangible, concrete gift where we can physically remember the goodness of the gospel story. Prayer of Confession Our worship moves from adoration to grace, to confession, then assurance, and finally to the Table. This prayer intentionally acknowledges our tendency toward self-reliance while anticipating the grace that Christ offers in the Sacrament.

The Church’s One Foundation In this hymn, written by Samuel Stone in 1866, we sing of Christ’s redeemed Church, who awaits the day when He will gather His people from every age and nation into eternal glory. Though the Church now struggles and suffers, Christ will preserve her until the final redemption, when she will dwell with Him forever in the new heavens and new earth.
