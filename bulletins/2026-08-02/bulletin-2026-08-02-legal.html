We Are Glad You Are Here!

We are honored that you decided to join us this morning. Whether it is your first time in church, your first time in a long while, or part of your regular rhythm, we hope you will be encouraged by the fellowship with each other, and with our Lord, as we worship him together.

Children & Nursery

Children (age 4+) and their families worship together in the sanctuary, where children will be dismissed to Children’s Church during the service. Children’s bulletins and crayons are available at both entrances to the Sanctuary. Children are always welcome in worship!

Nursery rooms for infants through age 3 are in the Education Wing, with the entrance behind the playground on the west side of campus. If you have a newborn and/or are nursing your baby, please feel free to use our baby room just off the narthex — a quiet space with comfortable seating where the worship service is broadcast as well.

Our Vision & Mission

Our vision is to see lives transformed by the power of the gospel, rooted in God’s Word and the Reformed tradition, in order to create a culture of discipleship at FPC where disciples WORSHIP holistically, GROW relationally in Christ, and SERVE boldly in the world for the glory of God. Our mission is to accomplish this by “Being Disciples and Making Disciples.”

Sermon Outline

“Restored by Grace: Consider Your Ways”

Rev. Waring Porter  ·  Ezra 5

Very few Christians intentionally decide to neglect Christ or abandon their faith. More often, we simply drift. Life becomes busy; responsibilities pile up; careers, children, hobbies, home projects, vacations, and endless notifications begin competing for our attention. None of those things are sinful in themselves. They are simply capable of becoming ultimate things instead of secondary things. But every so often the Lord graciously asks us a searching question: “Are you giving your best attention to the things that matter most?” That is exactly where God’s people find themselves in Ezra 5. The rebuilding of the Temple does not begin with new resources or a favorable political climate. It begins when God speaks. This leads us to the central truth of this passage: God renews His people through the faithful proclamation of His Word.

I. God’s Word Awakens Sleeping Hearts (vv. 1–2)

“The law of the LORD is perfect, reviving the soul.” — Psalm 19:7

II. God’s Word Produces Courageous Obedience (vv. 3–5)

“I am with you, declares the Lord.” — Haggai 1:13

III. God’s Word Advances God’s Kingdom (vv. 6–17)

“Semper reformanda secundum Verbum Dei” — always reforming, according to the Word of God

*The Hymn:  #277

“The Church’s One Foundation”
Hymn “The Church’s One Foundation” was written by Samuel J. Stone in 1866 to affirm the biblical doctrine of the Church during a time of theological controversy. It celebrates Christ as the Church’s only foundation, Savior, and Head — a truth we will see later in Ezra 5.

*Prayer of Invocation & The Lord’s Prayer

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy Name. Thy Kingdom come, thy will be done on earth, as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread, and forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors. Lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. For thine is the kingdom, the power, and the glory, forever. Amen.

Invocation To “invoke” is to call upon or earnestly seek. This must not be misunderstood to mean that we bring God into our presence — the Lord is already present with His people before our worship begins. We lift our voices to God, asking Him to draw near and enable us to worship Him rightly for His character and mighty works.

Now, therefore, thus says the Lord of hosts: Consider your ways. You have sown much, and harvested little. You eat, but you never have enough; you drink, but you never have your fill. You looked for much, and behold, it came to little. And when you brought it home, I blew it away. Why? declares the Lord of hosts. Because of my house that lies in ruins, while each of you busies himself with his own house. Therefore the heavens above you have withheld the dew, and the earth has withheld its produce. And I have called for a drought on the land and the hills, on the grain, the new wine, the oil, on what the ground brings forth, on man and beast, and on all their labors.”

Corporate Prayer of Confession

Gracious and Holy God, You have spoken by Your Word and called us to seek first Your kingdom and Your righteousness. Yet we confess that our hearts are prone to wander. Like Your people of old, we have too easily allowed distractions, fears, comforts, and worldly pursuits to crowd out wholehearted devotion to You. We confess that we have neglected Your Word, grown complacent in prayer, and pursued our own priorities more eagerly than Your purposes. Too often we have delayed obedience, settled for spiritual apathy, and failed to labor faithfully for the advancement of Your kingdom. Forgive us, O Lord. By Your Spirit, awaken us again through the faithful proclamation of Your truth. Renew our love for Christ, strengthen our faith, and restore in us a joyful eagerness to obey Your voice.

Silent Confession

Prayer of Confession God graciously reveals His holiness, and His people respond with humble confession, trusting His promise of forgiveness in Christ.

Assurance of Divine Pardon: Isaiah 55:10–11

“For as the rain and the snow come down from heaven and do not return there but water the earth, making it bring forth and sprout, giving seed to the sower and bread to the eater, so shall my word be that goes out from my mouth; it shall not return to me empty, but it shall accomplish that which I purpose, and shall succeed in the thing for which I sent it.”

Assurance of Pardon After confessing our sins, we hear God’s promise of forgiveness. True repentance is always answered by God’s faithful forgiveness, and Christ’s saving work is greater than our sin.
