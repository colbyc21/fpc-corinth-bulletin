Did You Know?

Yarn Crafters

 

Yarn Crafters makes hats and scarves for the warming center; hats, booties, and blankets for Oasis Medical Center; as well as layettes for newborns in the church body. We knit, crochet, and use a loom to create our goodies.

We have a multitude of projects and very few crafters. If you want to learn to knit or crochet, our experts are available to teach you!

Meetings: Tuesdays at 1:00 p.m. at Nancy Nunley’s house, 411 E. Fourth St. Call Janet Winders at 901-275-0765 for more information.

37th Community Thanksgiving Meal

Thanksgiving Morning · Thursday, November 26

Join us on Thanksgiving morning as we prepare and deliver 750 Thanksgiving meals to those who need it throughout Alcorn County. This is a HUGE project and can only be accomplished with the help of many. This is a great opportunity for the members of FPC to be disciples by “living and loving like Jesus.” You can help with meal prep or by delivering meals.

Please fill out the insert at church Sunday to volunteer your time, and place it in the offering plate or drop it by the church office.
