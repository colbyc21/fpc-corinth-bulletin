Encouragement from Bobby Capps: 

“You are the light of the world.” — Matthew 5:14
Light does not have to call attention to itself to be useful; it simply has to be present. Ask God to help you notice
the person already in front of you.

“Look not only to his own interests, but also to the interests of others.” — Philippians 2:4
Care starts with attention. Before we can help someone, we have to notice them.
