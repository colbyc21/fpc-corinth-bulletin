Our Vision & Mission

Our vision is to see lives transformed by the power of the gospel, rooted in God’s Word and the Reformed tradition, in order to create a culture of discipleship at FPC where disciples WORSHIP holistically, GROW relationally in Christ, and SERVE boldly in the world for the glory of God. Our mission is to accomplish this by “Being Disciples and Making Disciples.”

Sermon Outline

“Restored by Grace: God’s Providential Care”

Rev. Waring Porter  ·  Ezra 6:1–12

Imagine if the rebuilding of God’s house depended on locating one government record that had been filed away nearly twenty years earlier in one of the largest empires the world had ever known. If that document isn’t found, the rebuilding almost certainly comes to an end. God’s promises appear to be hanging by a thread. As we read the story, we might be tempted to think, “Well, I hope they get lucky.” But there is no luck in Ezra 6. There is only providence.

What is fascinating is that there are no dramatic miracles in this chapter. Instead, we watch what seems like an ordinary day inside a government office. On the surface, it looks like politics and paperwork. But behind every ordinary event stands the extraordinary hand of Almighty God. Ezra 6 reminds us that what appears to depend on earthly rulers is, in reality, governed by the sovereign Lord of heaven and earth. In Reformed theology, we call this God’s providential care.

WSC, Question 11: “What are God’s works of providence?” — “They are his most holy, wise, and powerful preserving and governing all his creatures, and all their actions.”

I. Directs the Decisions of Earthly Rulers (vv. 1–5)

II. Provides Everything Necessary for His Mission (vv. 6–10)

III. Protects His Covenant Purposes (vv. 11–12)

“Semper reformanda secundum Verbum Dei” — always reforming, according to the Word of God

*The Hymn:  #25

“Immortal, Invisible” (vv. 1–3)
Hymn Written by Walter Chalmers Smith in 1867, this hymn exalts God’s eternal majesty, wisdom, and sovereign rule over all creation. As we sing, we are reminded that the Lord is the unseen King who “reigns in light inaccessible” and accomplishes His perfect purposes through all earthly events. In Ezra 6, God even directs the decrees of pagan kings to protect and provide for His people, assuring us that His providential care always advances His kingdom and preserves His church.

Corporate Prayer of Confession

Sovereign and gracious Father, You rule over the nations, establish kingdoms, and accomplish all Your holy will. Yet we confess that we often doubt Your providence. We fear the power of people more than the power of God. We become anxious about tomorrow, seek to control what belongs in Your hands, and question Your wisdom when Your purposes are hidden from us. Forgive us for our unbelief and our pride. Grant us humble hearts that trust Your perfect rule, rest in Your faithful care, and gladly submit to Your will. Through Jesus Christ, our gracious King and Savior, we pray.

Silent Confession

Corporate Confession Our corporate confession of sin is a three-fold acknowledgement that the world is not the way it was meant to be; I am not the way I was meant to be; and we as a church are not the way we were meant to be. As we acknowledge our failures together, we testify to the world that the gospel is rooted in the faithful mercy of God alone. This is the most honest moment of our week, as we humbly agree with Scripture’s claim that all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.

*Profession of Faith: The Apostles’ Creed

I believe in God, the Father almighty, Maker of heaven and earth, and in Jesus Christ, his only Son, our Lord, who was conceived by the Holy Ghost, born of the Virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, dead and buried; he descended into hell; the third day he rose again from the dead; he ascended into heaven, and sitteth on the right hand of God the Father Almighty; from thence he shall come to judge the quick and the dead. I believe in the Holy Ghost, the holy catholic* Church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and the life everlasting. Amen.

*that is, the true Christian church of all times and all places

The Apostles’ Creed The Apostles’ Creed is a concise summary of the Christian faith, affirming the Triune God — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — and the redeeming work of Jesus Christ. It connects us with the historic church and provides a clear, biblical confession that strengthens our unity and directs our worship.
